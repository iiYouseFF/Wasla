# Supplier / Wholesaler Commercial Agreement — Draft Template

**Version:** 1.0 (Draft — NOT LEGALLY REVIEWED)
**Status:** Placeholder for legal counsel review before use

> ⚠️ **Important:** This is a structural outline to align product/business thinking on commercial terms before drafting a binding contract. It must be finalized by a lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction before being sent to any real Supplier or Wholesaler. It is not legal advice.

---

## 1. Parties
- Platform operating entity (legal name, registration details)
- Supplier/Wholesaler (business name, registration number, authorized signatory)

## 2. Purpose
Defines the commercial relationship under which the Supplier/Wholesaler lists products and fulfills orders through the Platform's marketplace.

## 3. Onboarding & Account Requirements
- Business registration and KYC document submission
- Platform's right to approve/reject/suspend accounts (reference Terms of Service)
- Accuracy of product listing information (price, stock, description) is Seller's responsibility

## 4. Commission & Fees

| Fee Type | Structure (placeholder — confirm actual rates with finance/leadership) |
|---|---|
| Transaction commission | X% of order subtotal per completed sub-order |
| Payment processing fee | Passed through from PSP, or absorbed by Platform (decide model) |
| Promotional placement fee (optional) | Flat fee or bid-based for featured listing placement |
| Delivery fee (if Platform-facilitated logistics used) | Per-delivery flat fee or distance-based |

**Fee changes:** Platform to provide advance notice (e.g., 30 days) before commission rate changes take effect.

## 5. Payment & Payout Terms
- Payout schedule (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, or net-15/net-30 after order completion)
- Payout method (bank transfer, wallet)
- Reconciliation process and dispute window for payout discrepancies
- Handling of refunds/returns impact on payout (deduction from next payout cycle vs. separate reconciliation)

## 6. Inventory & Fulfillment Obligations
- Seller must maintain accurate real-time stock levels
- Order acceptance/fulfillment SLA (e.g., must confirm order within X hours, dispatch within X hours of confirmation)
- Consequences of repeated fulfillment failures (order cancellation rate threshold triggering account review)

## 7. Product Quality & Compliance
- Seller warrants products are authentic, legally compliant, and accurately described
- Seller responsible for any regulatory certifications required for their product category (food safety, expiry date compliance, etc.)
- Platform's right to remove listings that violate quality/compliance standards

## 8. Delivery Responsibility
- Define whether Seller uses own fleet, Platform-arranged 3rd-party logistics, or a hybrid — this affects liability allocation for delivery delays/damages
- Packaging standards expected of Seller

## 9. Returns & Disputes
- Return/refund eligibility conditions
- Dispute escalation process between Retailer, Seller, and Platform
- Platform's role: facilitator/mediator (clarify Platform is not itself liable for product defects unless Platform is the merchant of record for certain SKUs)

## 10. Data & Confidentiality
- Seller's access to Platform data limited to their own sales/order data
- Confidentiality of commercial terms (both parties)
- Data ownership: Platform retains ownership of aggregated/anonymized marketplace data; Seller retains ownership of their own product IP/content

## 11. Term & Termination
- Contract term (e.g., ongoing, auto-renewing annually) and termination notice period
- Grounds for immediate termination (fraud, repeated compliance violations, non-payment of fees owed)
- Effect of termination on pending orders and outstanding payouts

## 12. Liability & Indemnification
- Standard mutual indemnification clauses (jurisdiction-dependent — legal counsel required)
- Liability caps

## 13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
- Jurisdiction and applicable law
- Arbitration/mediation clause if applicable

## Notes for Internal Business Team (Not Part of Final Contract)

- **Commission rate** should be benchmarked against comparable regional B2B marketplaces before finalizing — too high discourages seller adoption, too low undermines platform revenue model from the Business Plan
- Consider a tiered commission structure (lower rate for high-volume sellers) as a growth incentive
- Clarify early whether the Platform ever acts as merchant-of-record for any category (this significantly changes the liability section and may require different licensing)
- This template assumes a single generic contract; in practice, Supplier (brand/manufacturer) and Wholesaler (distributor) relationships may warrant separate contract variants given their different roles in the supply chain — flag this to legal counsel
