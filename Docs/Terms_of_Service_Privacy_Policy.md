## Part A: Terms of Service — Outline

### 1. Introduction & Acceptance of Terms
- Definition of "Platform," "we/us," "you/User"
- Scope: applies to Retailers, Suppliers, Wholesalers, and Sales Agents using the app/website
- Acceptance mechanism (checkbox at registration, "by using the app you agree...")

### 2. Eligibility
- Must be a registered business entity or authorized representative thereof (for Supplier/Wholesaler roles)
- Minimum age requirement for individual account holders
- Jurisdictions where the service is available

### 3. Account Registration & Verification
- Accuracy of information provided
- KYC/business verification requirement for Suppliers/Wholesalers
- Account suspension/termination conditions (fraud, false information, non-payment)

### 4. Marketplace Role & Liability
- **Platform as intermediary**: clarify the platform facilitates transactions between independent Retailers and Suppliers/Wholesalers; it is not itself the seller of goods (unless operating a direct-sale model for some SKUs — clarify per actual business model)
- Product quality/accuracy of listings is the responsibility of the Supplier/Wholesaler
- Platform's role in dispute mediation (facilitator, not guarantor, unless otherwise stated)

### 5. Ordering, Pricing & Payment
- Price display, currency, and tax handling
- Payment methods accepted (COD, card, wallet, BNPL) and processing via 3rd-party providers
- Order confirmation and cancellation policy
- Minimum order quantities (MOQ) as set by sellers

### 6. BNPL / Financing Terms
- **This section requires dedicated legal drafting** — BNPL involves consumer/commercial credit regulations specific to the target jurisdiction
- Credit limit determination and modification rights
- Repayment terms, late payment consequences, default handling
- Relationship disclosure if BNPL is provided via a 3rd-party fintech partner (the partner's terms may need to be separately incorporated/linked)

### 7. Delivery & Fulfillment
- Delivery timelines are estimates, not guarantees, unless otherwise stated
- Responsibility allocation for delivery delays/damages between Platform, Seller, and delivery partner

### 8. Returns, Refunds & Disputes
- Conditions for returns/refunds
- Dispute resolution process and escalation path
- Platform's discretion in mediating disputes between Retailer and Seller

### 9. Prohibited Conduct
- Fraudulent orders, fake accounts, price manipulation, counterfeit goods listing, abuse of promotions/referral programs

### 10. Intellectual Property
- Platform branding, app content ownership
- License granted to Sellers for uploading product images/descriptions

### 11. Limitation of Liability
- Standard limitation of liability clauses (jurisdiction-dependent — legal counsel required)

### 12. Termination
- Grounds for account termination by either party
- Effect of termination on pending orders/payouts

### 13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
- Jurisdiction and applicable law (to be set per country of incorporation/operation)
- Arbitration/mediation clause if applicable

### 14. Changes to Terms
- Notice period and method for terms updates

### 15. Contact Information

## Jurisdiction-Specific Flags for Legal Counsel

Given the target market context (referenced Egypt/MENA-style B2B FMCG model), flag these for local counsel specifically:

- **BNPL/lending licensing requirements** — many jurisdictions require a specific financial services license or partnership structure with a licensed lender to legally offer BNPL
- **Data localization laws** — some countries require certain categories of data (especially financial) to be stored on local servers
- **Consumer protection law applicability** — confirm whether B2B transactions between businesses fall under consumer protection statutes or a separate commercial code
- **E-commerce/electronic transaction laws** — requirements for e-signatures, digital contract validity, invoicing format
- **KYC/AML obligations** — especially relevant given the BNPL/financial services component
